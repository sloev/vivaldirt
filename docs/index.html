Recording: The Four Seasons – John Harrison with the Wichita State University Chamber Players, via the Free Music Archive / Internet Archive, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0. The audio here is that recording, with silence trimmed between movements, cut into lossless FLAC segments and time-stretched live in your browser.

Score: Vivaldi, Le quattro stagioni, Op. 8 Nos. 1–4 (Dover edition), public domain, from IMSLP #126435. The bar cursor is an estimate: bars are taken to be of equal length within each movement, except in Summer II, where it follows the Presto thunderclaps in the recording.
