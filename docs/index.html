About

Vivaldi’s Four Seasons, stretched across the year. Each concerto lasts exactly as long as its season: spring from 1 March, summer from 1 June, autumn from 1 September, winter from 1 December. The music is always at the present moment, one bar every few hours, time-stretched live in your browser with Paulstretch.

The building is made from the music. The system being played is engraved on its main face, the next one wraps around the corner, and the staff lines run on around the whole building as formwork lines. A cut in the facade marks the playhead. Each movement raises a different massing, and the cantilevers reach further as the movement unfolds. The perforations and the softness of the shadows follow the loudness of the recording. The sun is the real sun of this moment, at the season of the piece. Everyone who opens it at the same moment sees the same building.

Settings

The count is peer-to-peer (WebRTC via public Nostr relays), with no server of ours. As with any WebRTC connection, browsers in the room can see each other’s IP addresses. Only whether you are listening is shared. Turn it off and nothing connects.
