Sheet music

Antonio Vivaldi, Le quattro stagioni, Op. 8 Nos. 1–4 (RV 269, 315, 293, 297), edited by Eleanor Selfridge-Field, from “The Four Seasons” and Other Violin Concertos in Full Score, Dover Publications, New York, 1995. Scanned by archive.org; uploaded to IMSLP by Feduol (files #799159, #799160, #799162, #799163). Public domain per IMSLP.

The bar cursor is an estimate: bars are taken to be of equal length within each movement, except in Summer II, where it follows the Presto thunderclaps in the recording.
