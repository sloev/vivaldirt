Time travel

The music is always where the year is.

About

The idea

Antonio Vivaldi’s The Four Seasons (Op. 8, 1725) stretched across a full calendar year. Each concerto lasts exactly as long as its season. The playhead is always at the present moment in the year.

How it works

The recording is time-stretched in your browser with Paulstretch (by Nasca Octavian Paul), so a few minutes of music expand to roughly three months. Stretch factors are typically on the order of ×10 000. Audio is decoded segment by segment and stretched with a WebAssembly build of Paulstretch — nothing is pre-rendered for the whole year.
