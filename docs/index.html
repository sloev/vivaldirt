Vivaldi’s Four Seasons, stretched across the year. Each concerto lasts exactly as long as its season: spring from 1 March, summer from 1 June, autumn from 1 September, winter from 1 December. The music is always at the present moment, one bar every few hours, time-stretched live in your browser with Paulstretch.

The architecture is drawn from the score and the recording. It reorganises with each movement, opens up in quiet passages and closes in when the music is loud, follows the light of the day, and changes character with the seasons. Everyone who opens it at the same moment sees the same image.

Settings

The count is peer-to-peer (WebRTC via public Nostr relays), with no server of ours. As with any WebRTC connection, browsers in the room can see each other’s IP addresses. Only whether you are listening is shared. Turn it off and nothing connects.
